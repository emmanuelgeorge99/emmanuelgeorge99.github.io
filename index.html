About

I'm an early-career urban and regional planner with hands-on experience in residential layout planning, landscape design, site supervision and planning documentation. I trained at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, and have since worked across a federal planning authority, a landscape design firm, and a string of independent GIS and mapping projects.

My strength is moving between the drafting table and the field: preparing and modifying planning drawings in AutoCAD, interpreting spatial data in QGIS, and supervising how a plan actually gets built on site. I'm a two-time Top-10 finalist in the Power the Community International Design Competition, and led the team's 2026 entry.
